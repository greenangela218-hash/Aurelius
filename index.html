Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real financial platform?

No. NairaVault is a frontend-only prototype. All transactions, balances, and rewards are simulated. No real money is ever transferred, and no banking APIs are connected.

Do I need to provide real payment details?

No. You can explore the entire platform using demo data. We never request real PINs, OTPs, or card security codes.

How are rewards calculated?

Rewards are simulated based on the tasks you complete and the plans you join. They are clearly labelled as demo transactions throughout the platform.

Can I withdraw money?

You can simulate a withdrawal request, but no real funds are moved. The withdrawal record will show as a demo transaction.

Is my data safe?

This is a frontend prototype. Any information you enter is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) for demo purposes only and is not transmitted anywhere.

Need Support?

This is a demo. For questions about the prototype, reach out to the developer.
